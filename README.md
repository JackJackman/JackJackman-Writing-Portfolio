# JackJackman-Writing-Portfolio

I am a technical writer with a background in IT and research. While working as a developer at a research and innovation hub for an Irish university, I increasingly found myself writing documentation, project deliverables, academic papers, and funding proposals. 

I wrote about sustainability and the smart energy sector for a varied audience of technical and non-technical readers. I went on to study Technical Writing at the University of Limerick, and am now perusing a career as a technical communicator. 

I bring my technical and research experience to organise technical concepts into documentation that is tailored to the intended audience. With the advent of generative AI, I remain current with advances in technical communications, while placing human expression first and foremost.

Please take a moment to review [some samples of my work](https://github.com/JackJackman/JackJackman-Writing-Portfolio/blob/main/0%20--%20Manifest.md), and feel free to contact me at [jack@lambentwriter.com](mailto:jack@lambentwriter.com) with any inquiries.
